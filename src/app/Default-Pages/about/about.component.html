About Run For Rangers

Supporting Wildlife Conservation Through Fun, Impactful Events

Run For Rangers is a dynamic organization that combines the power of community running events with meaningful wildlife conservation action. We believe that every step taken by our runners contributes to protecting Africa's precious wildlife and supporting the brave rangers who risk their lives daily to safeguard our natural heritage.

750+ Active Runners
R3.2M Funds Raised
25 Conservation Projects
🦏

Our Mission

To support and fund anti-poaching efforts, ranger training programs, and wildlife conservation initiatives across Africa through community-driven fundraising events that unite runners worldwide in the fight against wildlife crime.

�

Our Vision

A future where Africa's wildlife thrives in protected environments, supported by well-equipped ranger teams and engaged communities who understand that wildlife conservation is essential for our planet's survival.

�️

Our Values

Courage, Conservation, Community, Transparency, and Action. We honor the bravery of rangers while building a global community committed to wildlife protection through meaningful engagement and direct support.

What We Do

Our comprehensive approach to supporting rangers and wildlife conservation across Africa

🏃‍♂️

Community Running Events

Organize engaging running events where participants raise funds for specific ranger units and anti-poaching operations, creating a direct connection between runners and conservation efforts.

�

Ranger Support Programs

Provide essential equipment, training, and support to ranger teams on the frontlines of wildlife protection, ensuring they have the tools needed to protect Africa's endangered species.

�

Anti-Poaching Technology

Fund advanced technology solutions including surveillance equipment, communication systems, and tracking devices to help rangers combat poaching more effectively.

🎓

Training & Education

Support comprehensive training programs for rangers and provide educational resources to local communities about wildlife conservation and sustainable practices.

🏥

Emergency Medical Support

Provide critical medical aid and insurance support for rangers who are injured in the line of duty protecting wildlife from poachers and other threats.

�

Digital Impact Platform

Connect runners directly with conservation projects through our platform, providing real-time updates on how their fundraising efforts are making a tangible difference.
